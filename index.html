A new chapter is taking shape

Something thoughtful is taking shape.

Koru is an emerging design company exploring the relationship between space, material, light, and the objects that shape our everyday lives.

Be Part of the BeginningDiscover Koru

A practice of thoughtful making.

We believe the most meaningful designs do more than occupy space. They shape how we experience it.

Koru is being built around an appreciation for thoughtful ideas, honest materials, functional beauty, and the details that make ordinary experiences extraordinary.

From the atmosphere of a room to the character of an object, we are interested in design that feels considered, purposeful, and enduring.

We are at the beginning of our journey, and this is where the story starts.
